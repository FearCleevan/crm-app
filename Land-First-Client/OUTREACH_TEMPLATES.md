# Outreach Templates

Rule for all of these: pick ONE lead service and ONE proof point per message, matched to the recipient (see the positioning matrix in `GAME_PLAN.md`). Fill in the `[ ]` fields — don't send generic.

---

## 1. Cold email / DM (your own hand-picked list)

Lead with a short self-introduction first — who you are and what you do —
before pointing out anything about their site. Going straight to "here's
what's wrong with your site" reads as cold/critical even when the observation
is accurate; introducing yourself first frames it as a professional reaching
out, not a stranger picking something apart.

Subject: `Quick note about [Company]'s website`

```
Hi [Name],

I'm Peter Paul Lazan, a full-stack developer based in the Philippines — I
build complete web platforms (storefronts, admin systems, CRMs, ordering)
for small and mid-size businesses across Canada and the US. Most recently
I designed and built the full platform for Acme Vintage Supply — storefront,
checkout, admin dashboard, CMS, and security — solo, in about seven weeks.

I was looking at [Company]'s site and noticed [specific, true, observable
problem — e.g. "the mobile menu overlaps the header on smaller screens" /
"product pages aren't showing up in Google search results" / "the checkout
redirects to a broken page on mobile"].

[One line on what you'd do about it, or offer to send a quick breakdown —
no obligation. If it's useful, we can go from there.]

[Your name]
[Portfolio link] · [WhatsApp link]
```

**Follow-up (send once, 4–5 days later if no reply):**
```
Hi [Name], following up in case this got buried — happy to send that
quick breakdown whenever's convenient. No worries either way.
```

## 2. Upwork proposal

```
Hi [Client name],

[First line: name the specific problem from their job post in your own words —
proves you read it, not a template.]

I'd approach this by [one sentence on your approach].

For context: I recently built [matching proof point — e.g. "a full e-commerce
platform end-to-end, including the admin dashboard and CMS, in about 7 weeks"
or "a multi-tenant CRM (Paul CRM — live at crm.peterpaullazan.com) handling
auth, real-time data, and role-based permissions"], so this kind of scope is
very much in my day-to-day.

I'm available to start [immediately / on X date] and can hop on a quick call
if useful to confirm scope before we start.

[Your name]
[Portfolio link]
```

**Entry-tier version (for your first 1–2 bootstrap jobs — Landing Page Sprint / small Site Rescue):**
Keep it just as specific about their problem, but lead with speed and price-to-win rather than the big case studies — you're optimizing for the review here, not the biggest possible scope.

## 4. Discovery-call / scope-confirmation reply (once someone responds)

```
Thanks for the reply, [Name] — glad it's useful.

A few quick questions so I can put together an accurate scope and price:
1. [Budget range question, phrased softly — e.g. "Do you have a rough
   budget range in mind for this?"]
2. [Timeline — when would you want this live?]
3. [Any must-have feature or constraint I should know up front?]

Once I have that I'll send over a short written scope + fixed price within
a day.
```
