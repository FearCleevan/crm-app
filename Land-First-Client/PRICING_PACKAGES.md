## Foot-in-the-door pricing strategy (first 1–3 clients only)

The goal for your very first clients is reviews and a real testimonial, not maximum margin. Deliberately underprice the low end of Landing Page Sprint or Site Rescue for the first 1–2 Upwork jobs specifically to secure fast, five-star-likely wins. Once you have 2–3 reviews:

1. Move pricing back up to the bands above for new Upwork bids
2. Start leading proposals with Web Application / System Design where Paul CRM is your proof
3. Stop taking bootstrap-priced jobs — the reviews have done their job

## Should pricing be shown on the live portfolio?

Two valid approaches — pick one deliberately:

- **Show "starting at $X"** — pre-qualifies leads, saves time on people outside your range, standard for productized services.
- **Keep it hidden** — forces a discovery call, works better for higher-ticket custom-scoped work (System Design, Web Application) where price genuinely depends on scope.

A reasonable middle ground: show starting prices for the fixed-scope services (Landing Page Sprint, Custom Website) and keep "scoped after discovery call" for the open-ended ones (Web Application, System Design, Mobile, Kiosk POS) — which is close to what `data/services.ts` already implies, just not stated in dollars yet.
